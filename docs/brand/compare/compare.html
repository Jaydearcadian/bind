Bind logo: dovetail, ring or pearl ring?

Three directions, each with the same parts: lockup, states, animated logo, loader, swipe and a hero. The third is the ring made ownable, keeping the Newsreader and Geist type and the ring's smoothness. Everything is shown at the same size.

Fairness note. The dovetail got the most iteration. The ring's loader, swipe and hero were added only for this comparison. The pearl ring is new and had one round of iteration. The dovetail's tagline is "Hand off the busywork."; the ring and pearl heroes use "Connect once. Get back to work."

6. Side by side, assessed

My assessment, not a measurement. Where a row says "tested", I ran it in a browser here.

QuestionDovetailRingPearl ring
What it saysTwo things joined cleanly; work changes hands.A loop that closes: a connection made.A ring that is closed by a single bead. The bead is also the dot of the i, so the logo and the name are one idea.
Ownable?High. An interlocking two-tone block is unusual in software. Some will see a clapperboard or an "H" first.Low. A ring with an accent is a common shape.Medium to high. It is a serif "o" with stroke contrast, which is rare for a mark, and it is built from the font you chose. Risk: a ring with a stone can read as jewellery.
SmoothnessSoft corners, but hard geometry.Very smooth: one even round-capped stroke.Smooth and elegant: curves with thick and thin, no hard joins.
Small sizesHolds at 16 px (tested).Holds at 16 px (tested), the latch becomes a speck.The hairline top and bottom thin out at 16 px (tested): it reads as a narrow "o" with a dot. Fine at 24 px and up. This is its weakest point.
Fit with the typeDifferent voice (Bricolage Grotesque).Geometric mark beside a serif wordmark: slightly mismatched.Best: the mark and the Newsreader wordmark are the same drawing language.
Status as a lightPiece offset and colour.Latch position and colour.Pearl lifted, seated or off to the side, with colour. A pearl is easier to see than a thin latch, but "waiting" and "refused" differ mainly by colour and a small sideways shift. Needs a colour-blind user test.
My recommendationThe boldest and most distinctive. Different type and palette, so adopting it means leaving the font you like.The baseline.Pearl ring, if you want the font and the smoothness and an ownable mark. Dovetail if you would rather have the most distinctive mark and can change the type.

Not tested for any of them: other operating systems' font rendering, colour-blind users, device performance, trademark clearance. The pearl ring has had less iteration than the dovetail.
