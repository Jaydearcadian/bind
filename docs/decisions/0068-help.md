# 0068: Help, and the app navigation complete

Status: Accepted. Follows the UX brief ("People, Settings, Help and the shell").

## Decision
- `/app/help` holds short answers for owners and staff (what Bind does, approving, limits and who enforces them, pausing, simulated, sent vs confirmed vs paid, verified live vs approved by you, keys, inviting, staff, Activity, builders, the demo). Each says only what Bind does today and links to the screen it is about.
- Search filters as you type, opens the answer when only one matches, and says plainly when nothing does. `/app/help#<topic>` opens that answer, clearing an active search first so a link never lands on a hidden answer (found by the test).
- No "Ask Bind": a confident wrong answer about spending limits would do harm (brief). "What is this?" tips beside hard terms are not built yet.
- Every app area is now a real page: Today, Activity, Team and limits, Connections, Business data, People, Settings, Help. No "Soon" items remain in the side navigation or the phone More menu.

## Evidence
`tests/web/help.spec.ts` (desktop and phone): search, single-match open, no-match text, a deep link after a search, a help link to Connections; every area reached from the side navigation on desktop and from the tab bar or More menu on a phone, each with its heading, and no "Soon" left. `LOCAL_PASS`.

## Not done
Inline "What is this?" tips, a public help page outside the app, and contact or support channels.
