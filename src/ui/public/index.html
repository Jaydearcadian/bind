THE THREEFOLD BIND

Context. Authority. Fulfilment.

Agents are only useful in a real business when three things stay connected: what the business actually stocks and prefers, what an agent is allowed to commit, and what physically arrived at the back door.

1. Business Context

Par levels, pack sizes, preferred suppliers, and operating schedules live in structured workspace state—so the agent works from real inventory numbers, not guesses.

SKUs · Par thresholds · Approved vendors

2. Bounded Authority

Deterministic backend policy checks enforce daily budgets, vendor allowlists, and owner approval gates outside the language model before any order or transfer is sent.

Deterministic policy · Owner approval gates

3. Real-World Fulfilment

A settled transaction on Arc Testnet is evidence of payment, not proof of delivery. Bind keeps physical delivery receipts and three-way invoice matching separate.

PO + Delivery Receipt + Bill 3-way match

GOVERNED WORKFLOW

From low stock to a decision.

When kitchen stock drops below par, Bind’s Replenishment Agent reads workspace rules, requests supplier quotes, and submits a structured proposal. Deterministic code—not prompt trust—decides whether the order can proceed automatically or waits for the owner.
