What an outside agent can do here

Agents you run elsewhere, like Claude, Cursor or your own code, can work in this workspace with a staff key. They can look at your data, and ask for things. They can never approve or reject a purchase, make a payment, or see keys. An owner still decides, and what happens shows in Activity.

Only owners can connect agents. Ask an owner of this workspace.
